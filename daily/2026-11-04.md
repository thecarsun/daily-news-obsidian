## 2. Roads Agency vows to sort Cross traffic lights
- **Source:** mywelshpool_co_uk
- **Link:** https://www.mywelshpool.co.uk/newsviewer/ArticleId/30331/Roads-Agency-vows-to-sort-Cross-traffic-lights
- **Summary:** There’s good news for motorists passing through the town centre after the Trunk Roads Agency vowed to fix the sequencing problems causing by the Cross traffic lights. Following a number of near misses, MyWelshpool highlighted the issues last week after it was also raised at a Welshpool Town Council meeting. We raised the issues with the Trunk Roads Agency who have confirmed this morning that they are on the case. They told us that the operation of the traffic signals will be reviewed, and any appropriate changes will be undertaken. In addition, over the next few weeks they will be introducing additional technology to optimise traffic flows, adjust timings, and reduce congestion. The lights were reconnected this summer after being out of order for eight months following an underground fire in January.

## 4. Rajkumar Hirani takes a dig at films and shows UNNECESSARILY loaded with abuses; says, “You'll remember a show for its characters, plot, theme and not because it had 200 GAALIS”
- **Source:** bollywoodhungama
- **Link:** https://www.bollywoodhungama.com/news/features/rajkumar-hirani-takes-a-dig-at-films-and-shows-unnecessarily-loaded-with-abuses-says-youll-remember-a-show-for-its-characters-plot-theme-and-not-because-it-had-200-gaalis/
- **Summary:** Rajkumar Hirani and Abhijat Joshi had a conversation with Komal Nahta about their memorable films. Their films are known for being clean and family friendly while we are living in times when films or shows often have lots of abuses. Rajkumar Hirani was asked about it and how he views this trend. Rajkumar Hirani said, "Film ka har character apni zubaan bolta hain. For instance, Rancho (Aamir Khan) in 3 Idiots (2009) talks in a certain way while Raju's (Sharman Joshi) vocabulary would be a bit different as he comes from a different strata. Chatur (Omi Vaidya) doesn't know Hindi properly and his language would be different. So, if you character comes from a certain section of society where abusing is very common, then it's valid (to have abuses in those scenes)." Rajkumar Hirani added, "But if you add abuses as it's in trend or that you feel that logon ko accha lagega, but it doesn't work that way. People might feel 'Yeh zyada bol raha hain. Yahaan tak toh theek tha lekin yeh toh har sentence mein gaali de raha hain'." Rajkumar Hirani further said, "There's no resistance to it. But unnecessarily thinking that gaali dekh ke log bolenge 'Waah, kamaal ka show dekha. Itni gaaliyan sunne ko mili'! It may temporarily work but ultimately, you'll remember the story, the characters, the plot, the theme, etc. You won't remember 'iss show mein 200 gaaliyan thi. Bahut hi maza aa gaya' (laughs)!”

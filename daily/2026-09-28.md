## 3. Iran has no plans for new talks with US in NY: State media
- **Source:** aaj_tv
- **Link:** https://english.aaj.tv/news/330474327/iran-has-no-plans-for-new-talks-with-us-in-ny-state-media
- **Summary:** Iran has no plans to hold a new round of negotiations with the United States in New York, a source close to Iran’s UN delegation said, even as indirect contacts between the two sides continue through mediators. The source, speaking to IRNA on condition of anonymity, said Iran had conveyed its positions to Washington last Tuesday through Qatari mediation and was awaiting a US response. “The considerations and positions of the Islamic Republic of Iran were conveyed clearly and explicitly to the American side last Tuesday through the Qatari mediator, and Iran is awaiting the American side’s response,” the source said. “Accordingly, there is no plan for a new round of negotiations with the American side on the agenda of Iran’s delegation, and the foreign minister will return home on Tuesday after completing his scheduled programmes,” the source added. Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian and Abbas Araghchi are in New York for the 81st session of the UN General Assembly. Meanwhile, Axios reported that “positive and constructive” US-Iran discussions were continuing through mediators, including on nuclear issues, despite US President Donald Trump rejecting Tehran’s latest proposal. The report said talks remained possible, with Qatar emerging as the main intermediary. Qatari Prime Minister Sheikh Mohammed bin Abdulrahman Al Thani met Pezeshkian in New York last Thursday and discussed a potential deal with the Trump administration, according to Axios.

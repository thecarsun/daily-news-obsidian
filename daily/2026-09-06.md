## 3. President Zardari, PM Shehbaz pay tribute to martyrs
- **Source:** aaj_tv
- **Link:** https://english.aaj.tv/news/330471079/president-zardari-pm-shehbaz-pay-tribute-to-martyrs
- **Summary:** President Asif Zardari and Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif on Sunday paid tribute to the martyrs and veterans of Pakistan’s Armed Forces and their families, reaffirming the country’s resolve to protect its sovereignty, security and territorial integrity. In their separate messages on the occasion of Defence and Martyrs’ Day, the leaders said the day remained a defining chapter in Pakistan’s history, recalling the unity, courage and sacrifices displayed by the nation and armed forces during the 1965 war. President Zardari said the martyrs had written “timeless stories of courage and patriotism”, adding that their sacrifices would continue to guide future generations. Prime Minister Shehbaz described September 6 as a reminder of the “unparalleled resolve, unity and sacrifices” demonstrated in defence of the country. The leaders said the professionalism, preparedness and courage displayed by Pakistan’s armed forces in 1965 had created an enduring legacy, while the nation’s unity and resilience had strengthened the country’s defence. They said the spirit of September 6 was reflected again in the success of Operation Bunyanum Marsoos, which, they maintained, had added another chapter to Pakistan’s military history and demonstrated the preparedness and professionalism of the armed forces. President Zardari said the operation represented a continuation of the patriotism and resolve associated with Defence Day, while Prime Minister Shehbaz said the armed forces had delivered a response to Pakistan’s “age-old adversary” that would be remembered for generations. The prime minister said, “The challenges confronting Pakistan today demand greater national unity and solidarity. Our traditional adversary, along with Fitna Al Khawarij and Fitna Al Hindustan, continues to pursue its nefarious designs against Pakistan, but these hostile forces are facing successive and decisive defeats. The sinister designs of hostile forces seeking to destabilise Pakistan through hybrid warfare shall be resolutely defeated.” Both leaders stressed the need for greater national unity in confronting security challenges, including hostile efforts to destabilise Pakistan through conventional and hybrid means. The president and prime minister also emphasised that national strength extended beyond military capability, calling economic stability, political tolerance, education, development and social cohesion essential to building a strong Pakistan. Prime Minister Shehbaz said the government would continue measures aimed at strengthening national security and defence while advancing economic development, political stability and public welfare. The two leaders reiterated the country’s commitment to peace, dialogue and regional stability, saying Pakistan is a responsible state and a contributor to collective security beyond its borders. They also highlighted the Makkah Joint Defence Agreement as a manifestation of Pakistan’s growing role in regional security and its commitment to collective defence and cooperation with friendly countries. On the Kashmir dispute, both leaders said lasting peace in South Asia could not be achieved without its resolution in accordance with relevant UN Security Council resolutions and the aspirations of the Kashmiri people. The leaders called on the nation to uphold the principles of faith, unity and discipline and work towards transforming Pakistan into a peaceful, stable, prosperous and developed country. Both leaders prayed for the elevation of the ranks of the martyrs and for peace, stability and prosperity in Pakistan.
